About Quiz Zone

A simple, free platform for learning, practicing, and testing your knowledge.

Quiz Zone is a free, no-login quiz platform created to make learning and practice simple and accessible. It covers topics such as Developer/IT, General Knowledge, Mathematics, Science, General Aptitude, Exam Preparation, and Interview Preparation, with new topics and questions added regularly.

The platform is intentionally lightweight and does not require user accounts or a database. Quizzes are managed through JSON files, making it easy to add new topics, questions, and difficulty levels without changing the core application.

Technology Behind Quiz Zone

Quiz Zone is built using Java, Spring Boot, Thymeleaf, Bootstrap, Maven, and JSON-based quiz data. The application is deployed online using Docker and Render.
